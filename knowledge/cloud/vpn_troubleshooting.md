
# VPN Troubleshooting Runbook

## Purpose
This runbook provides safe first-level troubleshooting guidance for employees who cannot connect to the corporate VPN.

## Initial Checks
1. Confirm the employee has a working internet connection.
2. Verify the VPN client is installed and opens normally.
3. Check whether the employee is using the approved VPN connection profile.
4. Confirm the employee's system date and time are correct.
5. Ask the employee to note the exact VPN error message.

## Authentication Problems
- Verify the employee is using the correct corporate account.
- Check whether multi-factor authentication prompts are arriving.
- If the account is locked, escalate to the authorized identity support team.
- Never ask employees to share passwords or MFA codes.

## Connection Problems
- Check whether other websites are reachable.
- Confirm the VPN service status using the organization's approved status page.
- Try reconnecting through the approved VPN client.
- Do not disable antivirus, endpoint protection, or firewall controls.

## Escalation
Escalate to the network or security team if the issue persists, authentication fails repeatedly, or a security warning appears.

Record the error message, time of failure, VPN client version, and troubleshooting steps already attempted.
